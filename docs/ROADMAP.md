## What we are cutting

**Local wins:** the invented payoff table carries too much of the lesson. Stop polishing this version. Revisit only with a substantially better way to construct and challenge causal relationships, costs and incentives.

**Exploration versus delivery:** allocating generic effort invites optimisation of abstract coefficients. A future replacement should centre on choosing hypotheses, experiments and delivery bets, showing what each teaches and when it changes a decision. A replacement is not currently queued.

Three Answers, Disagreement, Questions, Territory and Family Tree lose their standalone status only when their useful capabilities and existing work have a functioning home. Consolidation is not permission to delete them first.

**Intervention Ladder (S05) is retained as Intervention workbench:** its focused pass now connects explicit causal relationships to expected observations, rival explanations and compared tests. The six intervention types remain optional prompts. Reframing explores interpretations; this workbench designs a test of a proposed relationship. The collection stays at 17.

## Reliability depth pass

Build on the existing dependency canvas and BESS example rather than adding a duplicate scenario. Compare several improvements, including redundancy, component improvements, simpler dependencies and fallback arrangements. Make any cost or effort assumptions explicit and editable; do not imply calibrated investment advice.

Explain why an improvement works through the dependencies it changes or retains. Clarify what the availability figures refer to; do not silently introduce repair times, switching behaviour or other dynamics absent from the model. Capture the decision, critical assumptions and unanswered questions alongside the comparison.

Success: a ten-minute session helps Matthew prepare a real product discussion and produces a better question or a changed decision.

## Rules for each increment

- Organise entry points around the question someone wants to explore. Reveal detail progressively; avoid making a large form the price of entry.
- Moving, connecting, assigning or sequencing must change the reasoning. Retain labelled keyboard, click and touch alternatives, usable scrolling and focus through edits.
- Preserve routes and stored-work formats. Consolidation must retain original material, alternatives, ancestry, Undo and portable exports; verify migration with existing saved examples before retiring an entry point.
- Carry an idea’s context and source when moving it between tools. Keep shared changes limited to what their actual consumers need.
- Use exposed, challengeable mechanisms and matched scenarios. Test consequential mechanism changes and exercise changed journeys in the actual app on desktop and phone.

Update an item to **In progress**, **Implemented**, **Published** or **Deferred** as appropriate. Implemented means the relevant checks passed; Published means deployment succeeded. Record the date and a link to delivery evidence with completed work. Record Matthew’s feedback and any changed decision here, keeping one current plan rather than appending competing roadmaps.

## Findings affecting the plan

2 October: the corrected Predictions model confirms that noiseless shared and separate estimates are identical when their starting values and update rates match. Keep this counterexample visible. A single noise sample must not become another predetermined lesson; users can replay other samples. Old saves are retained separately because correcting the mechanism changes their results.

2 October: Reliability comparisons need a shared definition of a successful service request. Added that definition to the model and each saved design; withhold gains across different definitions. Effort remains an editable assumption rather than a computed recommendation. The fallback example explicitly includes its own measurement dependency. These changes deepen the existing static model without claiming to simulate switching or repair.

2 October: question inversion should not manufacture an opposite to an interrogative sentence. New branches retain their parent and ask the user to name the premise being challenged. The combined Reframing board connects questions to frames as opening or challenging them, with detail opened progressively. Question-to-frame and frame-to-frame creation retain source snapshots; this is the first concrete use of shared ancestry intent, without prematurely retiring Family Tree.

Next: make the selective Accuracy improvements, inspect Delay without adding unnecessary scope, and audit the delivered roadmap.

2 October: Matthew prioritised matching his personal website before further consolidation, and authorised extending that language where the tools need it. The website repo supplies Oswald/Newsreader, warm paper and purple, and charcoal/lime dark mode. Add shared semantic colours for model states, retain the useful working geometry, and make this the foundation for remaining roadmap work.

2 October: the alternatives consolidation now keeps one collection across generation, concerns and opposing proposals. A comparison can show up to four options at a time without deleting the rest. Borrowed benefits and branches retain snapshots; source changes request review without rewriting prior reasoning. Copying earlier workspaces preserves an unchanged source record and leaves original saved bytes alone. Criteria stay visible in a compact, expandable summary so comparison is reached sooner.

2 October: Mixer and Territory now use one collection, with generation and coverage as views. Any two dimensions can form a map; gap notes survive switching or transposing axes. Moving or renaming a classification requests a fit review while preserving written mechanisms and original ingredients. Gap-led candidates retain the note that prompted them. Legacy captures only gain placements from exact, unambiguous label matches. Territory copies retain partial placements and full-length writing; dimensions still holding concepts or notes cannot be deleted silently.

2 October: Family Tree’s generation view, parking, branching and deliberate two-parent combinations now live in Alternatives and Mixer through shared snapshot and rendering helpers. Alternatives accepts all 24 concepts from an earlier tree, including original parent wording, reasons, ingredients, parked status and comparison selection; its collection limit is now 32. Reframing exposes an explicit reason for branching and the full source record. Branch snapshots are flat, so deep histories do not recursively inflate each new child. Oversized derived work is rejected before replacing saved work. The remaining scaffold passes will apply this capability to their own meaningful variations.

2 October: Flexibility’s useful counterfactual is local feasibility at actual stock and time. Releasing a promise in the explorer must not pretend to rewind earlier energy use or refund revenue. The explorer identifies all smallest release sets and distinguishes joint constraints from physical impossibility. Perfect one-hour price notice is an explicit experimental condition, with an exposed preparation rule shared by both commitment policies; future offers stay hidden. Day 2 changes the open policy’s result, while day 14 changes neither policy. Keep these counterexamples instead of implying that extra information always changes a decision. Full historical branching is unnecessary for this increment; existing whole-day replays remain the way to change earlier actions.

2 October: Commitment timing must vary only the chosen lever, including when other future policies are already scheduled. The new focused view freezes that reference and preserves the custom course separately. Its causal trace shows computed intermediate quantities without claiming to decompose the total effect. Capacity is explicitly the following week’s consequence of ending fatigue. Equal start weeks agree; under lighter demand an earlier reduction in promises loses useful output. Keep declined work beside output, and keep detailed policy construction available below the first view.

2 October: Teams’ editable presets preserve their exact seeded jobs. Saved arrangements freeze membership and names, while active demand and shared assumptions re-evaluate every arrangement together. Keep unfinished work beside completed-only lead time. Requested effort versus full-period capacity is a diagnostic, not evidence that capacity is usable at the right moment. Headcount and specialist skills remain fixed; this pass makes workload hypotheses challengeable without implying calibrated staffing advice.

2 October: returning a constraint to the board is not itself a feasibility check. Constraints now separates the useful principle from the imagined condition and compares several practical adaptations against all recorded limits. Fit is an explained judgement, not a score. Changed wording invalidates the old verdict when reviewed; new limits remain visibly unchecked, and removed limits keep their source record. This gives ancestry a specific job: preserve why a practical variation arose and what it assumed.

2 October: Analogy needs a target interaction in its own terms, not only a verdict on the source sentence. Conditions and failure points now travel into saved adaptations, which explicitly keep, redesign or omit each relationship. Different sources can inform alternatives in the same comparison. Their source records stay fixed; trying a changed mapping creates a new branch with undecided responses. This preserves a useful contrast with Constraints: Analogy tests borrowed relationships, while Constraints tests adaptations against actual limits.

2 October: Scenes fit judgements belong to a particular scene and intervention version, not to an idea in the abstract. Branches retain original wording; later context changes preserve the verdict but flag it for review. The comparison exposes person, information and placement, while a changed action is labelled as a different version. Copying an intervention to a new scene resets its review. Keep choosing a rehearsal distinct from claiming that a hypothesis has been validated.

2 October: Knowledge now tests several ways to distribute the same requested coaching time. Concurrent periods share finite person-time; teaching uses knowledge available at the start of the week, so relay learning takes time. Two backups beat one in the default absence scenario, but that ranking reverses when the specialist stays. Preserve that counterexample and the no-benefit cost of coaching already proficient people. Pinned plans retain starting skills and assignments while demand and absence remain shared; differing starting skills are explicitly flagged.

2 October: Exceptions needs independent effort estimates, not a silent conversion of value points into days. A finite quarter must fit before settlement; otherwise neither value nor core progress is earned. Core work progresses through a named queue, while its value remains unpriced. Both automatic rules exceed the default budget, so their comparison reports the first infeasible quarter rather than an impossible winning total. With enough spare capacity, upkeep displaces no available core work. Earlier saved accounting remains intact until an explicit capacity replay.

2 October: Adoption needs to distinguish partner mix from partner count. The new mixed ring holds every person’s degree at five, yet the same three pilots produce a different cascade from the dense groups. Equal totals can also hide disjoint adopters, so compare identities at the same round. Hub/custom networks may change degree and the comparison exposes changed assumptions. Isolated people get no coordination utility; repeating cycles retain their phase instead of being presented as stable outcomes.

2 October: Intervention workbench earns a separate place by moving from classification to causal test design. Explicit directed links retain conditions, timing, rival explanations and observations; changing step order changes layout only. Earlier arrows migrate as blank suggested hypotheses, not evidence. Branches and context-review snapshots retain prior reasoning, while changed relationships or tests request review. Keep six intervention types as optional prompts and distinguish choosing a test from validating its claim.
