# Thinking Lab

Build four things worth playing with, then use the experience to decide which deserve development. A portfolio of distinct interactions matters more than feature count.

| Experiment | Core question | Distinct interaction |
|---|---|---|
| The commitment spiral | How does making promises change our ability to keep them? | Fork a team simulation and intervene at different times |
| Where flexibility gets trapped | Which future actions does a battery commitment remove? | Play an uncertain operating day and compare policies |
| Reframing workbench | What changes when we describe the same problem differently? | Develop several frames and compare their interventions |
| Possibility mixer | What can an unfamiliar combination make possible? | Edit dimensions, lock ingredients, develop a concept |
