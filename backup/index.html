What travels with a backup?

Each address keeps separate browser storage. Download on the old address first, then import on the address where you use the tool. Product and Lab work can move to Tools Lab; Energy work stays on the Energy address. A mixed file can be used at both destinations.

Unsaved edits and models held only in a page URL need their own share link or tool export. Appearance settings, Gauge participant identity and live-session drafts are excluded. Nothing is sent to a server.

Backup files contain your work as readable text. Keep them somewhere appropriate for their contents. A recovery copy preserves damaged saved data without attempting to repair it.
