Instruments
for judgement

Small, sharp tools for product work under uncertainty.

01

Roadmap

Now / next / later columns, WIP limits, deck export — the plan as an artefact, not a promise.

→ 02

Timeline

Milestones with P50–P90 whiskers and a merge-risk verdict; the snapshot compare draws the slip slide.

→ 03

Bets

The quarter as a blotter of stakes, odds and payoffs — concentration risk included.

→ 04

Rank

Weighted-criteria ranking with a robustness shuffle — does #1 survive your own uncertainty?

→ 05

Duel

Pairwise showdowns when a list is too long to rank honestly in one pass.

→ 06

Map

Two axes, labelled zones, presets — position the portfolio and argue about placement, not vibes.

→ 07

Tree

Probability-weighted decision trees with expected value carried up the branches.

→ 08

Why

Outcome → assumptions → solutions; the argument drawn so the weak link is visible.

→ 09

Wardley

Value-chain evolution maps — what to build, buy, or let commoditise.

→ 10

Premortem

Write the failure story first, price it into an EV register, promote the real risks.

→ 11

Fermi

Driver-tree estimation with ranges that confess what must be true for the answer to hold.

→ 12

Gauge

Collect a room's estimates before anyone anchors it — Delphi rounds, chips, calibrated ranges.

→ 13

Alarm

Base-rate playground — how often a monitor that's “99% right” cries wolf.

→ 14

Flow

Little's-law throughput sketches — where the queue actually is.

→ 15

Signal vs noise

Eight noisy quarters, one real shift — Deming's tampering and regression to the mean, made felt.

→ 16

Paths

The questions inside the plan — what each answer buys, what happens either way, and whether the question can be answered at all.

→ 17

Proxy Hunt

Stress-test whether a metric under pressure can quietly harm the outcome you want or one you need to protect.

Case file
