    Instruments

    Bring a decision. Leave with something you can show.

    1. Size it

      FermiBuild a driver-tree estimate with ranges and see which assumptions matter.
      • GaugeCollect independent estimates, compare ranges and run another round.

    2. Choose

      RankRank options against weighted criteria and see whether the order survives uncertainty.
      • DuelCompare options in pairs when a list is too long to rank in one pass.
      • TreeDraw a decision tree with probabilities and expected values carried up its branches.
      • BetsCompare stakes, odds and payoffs across a portfolio of bets.
      • MapPlace a portfolio on two axes and explore what different positions mean.

    3. Plan it

      RoadmapArrange work across now, next and later, set WIP limits, and export the plan.
      • TimelineMap milestones, uncertainty ranges and dependencies; compare how the plan has moved.
      • PathsMap the questions inside a plan, what their answers buy and what happens either way.

    4. Test the argument

      PremortemWrite the failure story, estimate its risks and decide which ones need action.
      • WhyConnect outcomes, assumptions and solutions to expose the argument behind a plan.
      • Proxy HuntStress-test whether pressure on a metric can harm the outcomes it is meant to serve.
      • WardleyMap a value chain and its evolution to explore what to build, buy or commoditise.

    5. Make the case

      Case fileBring tool links, a decision question and a verdict together in one case.

    Models

    Play a mechanism for ten minutes.

    • FlowExplore the relationship between work in progress, throughput and waiting time.
    • AlarmExplore how base rates change the meaning of a supposedly accurate alarm.
    • The commitment spiralCan a team promise its way into losing the ability to deliver?
    • Steering through delayKeep a system steady while your instrument reports an earlier state.
    • How teams fit the workMove capabilities across team boundaries. Watch the work find a different route.
    • What survived the status update?Carry facts through reporting layers. Discover which omission changes a decision.

    Workbenches

    Find a different way into a problem.

    • Reframing workbenchDescribe the same problem differently. Notice what you would do next.
    • Possibility mixerCombine ingredients, map your concepts, and investigate spaces you have not explored.
    • Constraint playgroundChange a limit in your imagination. Bring the useful part back to reality.
    • Analogy workshopBorrow a mechanism from somewhere else. Find where the comparison breaks.
    • Intervention workbenchWhich causal relationship would you change, and what would you expect to observe?
    • Alternatives workbenchStart with an idea, an objection or a disagreement. Compare different ways to make it work.
    • Scene-first inventionInvent for a person at a particular moment, with partial information.
