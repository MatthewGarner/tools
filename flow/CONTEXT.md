# Flow context

## Terms

- **Flow check** is a one-stage queue model. It explains how demand, capacity, WIP and variability affect waiting; it does not schedule work or select strategy.
- **Expedite lane** is a deliberately exceptional priority path. Its cost is the additional waiting imposed on standard work when scarce capacity is reserved for expedites. It is not a faster team or free throughput.
- **Dice game** is a dependent-stations model. Uneven local output propagates through a serial system, so local average capacity does not guarantee system throughput. It is an intuition pump, not a forecast of a named team.
- **Batch economics** and **queue triage** are existing local lenses. They share Flow’s current input world but answer separate operational questions.

## Boundaries

Flow exposes operational trade-offs, not a roadmap, decision receipt, probability tree, or portfolio allocation. Its exports must state the modelled condition and the omitted real-world causes rather than presenting a universal delivery forecast.
