# Paths semantics

Paths is a conditional plan, not a task register. `decision` headings name the
questions whose evidence selects work; `when:` determines whether a later
question applies, rather than adding another condition to a work item.

An `answer` is a recorded decision receipt. An `assume` is a temporary planning
basis and must remain visibly distinct. Completed work is history even if the
condition that once selected it would now resolve differently.

`learn:` and `enough:` are an authored learning contract. Consumers may rank or
display it, but must not invent experiments, evidence, or outcome claims.

Tree and Plans enumerate possible work from the canonical evaluator. They must
not suppress condition repairs, turn an AND into an OR, or present refused plan
enumeration as a complete result.
