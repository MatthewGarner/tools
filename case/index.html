    Why a binder, and why one URL?

    Every tool on this site keeps its whole state in its URL — a fermi model, a decision tree, a delivery timeline, each one a bookmarkable claim. That's the superpower, and its cost: a real decision uses four of them, and they scatter across chat threads and documents until nobody can find the version that mattered. The case file composes what the URLs already carry.

    Assembly is deliberately manual: do the work in the member tools, then paste each URL here as an exhibit line with a label, a lane and a note saying why it matters. The cover renders as you type — the question as the standfirst, the exhibits as a register grouped by lane, and the verdict once you commit to one. A case never computes its verdict; the exhibits argue, you decide, and the cover states it.

    The case's own URL then carries the whole kit — one link for the board pack, the handover, the retro six months later. Exhibit rows are live links, so the reader lands on the actual model, not a screenshot of it. Exports keep the register but leave the URLs in the document, where links belong.

    No accounts, no tracking. The URL carries your case — bookmark or share it.
