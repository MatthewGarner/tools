# Case: authority and evidence

A Case is an authored argument and its review history, not an aggregate engine.
`decision` is the commitment authorised now; `unresolved` is deliberately separate.
Approving learning does not resolve the rollout decision. A later review records
what changed and its implication without rewriting the earlier authorisation.

A claim's basis distinguishes observation, assumption, model output and judgement.
These labels are authored classifications, not endorsements. A captured reference
means meaningful state decoded from the exact stored URL; it does not certify the
member model or turn a conditional forecast into observed evidence. Case never
fetches external references. Preserve planning receipts: Paths answers and assumed
Roadmap outcomes are different, and Timeline's lanes may represent jointly required
work rather than competing alternatives. Case must not infer probability or
agreement from exhibit count, score, layout or link validity.
