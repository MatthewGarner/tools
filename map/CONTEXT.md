# Map

Map is a two-axis, zone-based categorisation instrument. A preset supplies the
meaning of its axes, zones, advice and warnings; the generic renderer does not make
those methods interchangeable. Positions are rough claims, not measurements.

The only outbound handoff is from flagged **assumption maps** to Gauge, where it
collects independent room priors. A room prior is neither a designed test nor new
evidence. Risks, stakeholders, skills, RAG status and futures have their own
operating follow-up and must not be coerced into probability questions.
