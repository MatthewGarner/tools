# Why

Why is a causal-discovery instrument: outcome → opportunity → solution → assumption.
Its Causal Field makes every authored claim and its ancestry legible; its Delivery
Lens shows readiness derived from discovery status. Neither is a capacity plan,
commitment schedule, or decision plan. Roadmap owns delivery commitments; Paths owns
contingent investment decisions, evidence and outcomes. A `holds`/`broken` tag is the
tree's current claim, not an evidence receipt.

The Field is source-ordered and exhaustive in live views. The Lens is a factual
ledger: delivering, testing, unaddressed, and a separate no-why integrity exception;
candidate, shipped, and parked remain outside derived readiness. Exports are complete
16:9 plates or explicitly refuse in favour of the exhaustive native SVG—never select a
preferred branch. Only a broken claim uses alert colour; every other state is words and
quiet geometry.
