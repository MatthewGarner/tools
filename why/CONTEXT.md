# Why

Why is a causal-discovery instrument: outcome → opportunity → solution → assumption.
Its two views preserve that one model: the OST supports discovery; the delivery lens
shows readiness derived from discovery status. It is not a capacity plan, commitment
schedule, or decision plan. Roadmap owns delivery commitments; Paths owns contingent
investment decisions, evidence and outcomes. A `holds`/`broken` tag is the tree's
current claim, not an evidence receipt.
