# Why

Why is a causal-discovery instrument: outcome → opportunity → solution → assumption.
Its Causal Tree makes every authored claim and direct ancestry legible in a rooted,
left-to-right topology; its Delivery Lens shows readiness derived from discovery
status. Neither is a capacity plan,
commitment schedule, or decision plan. Roadmap owns delivery commitments; Paths owns
contingent investment decisions, evidence and outcomes. A `holds`/`broken` tag is the
tree's current claim, not an evidence receipt.

The Tree is exhaustive in its native canvas; phone projects the same source as a
source-order outline with full breadcrumbs rather than shrinking the desktop tree.
Outcomes form strong roots, opportunities and solutions use progressively smaller
neutral planes, and assumptions remain individually editable claims in ruled solution
bands. The Lens is a factual ledger: delivering, testing, unaddressed, and a separate
no-why integrity exception; candidate, shipped, and parked remain outside derived
readiness. Exports are complete 16:9 plates or explicitly refuse in favour of the
exhaustive native SVG—never select a preferred branch. Only a broken claim uses alert
colour; every other state is words and quiet geometry.
