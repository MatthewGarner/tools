# Proxy Hunt

Proxy is a causal-guardrail review of a measure under pressure. The `proxy` is
a measurement only: it never becomes a causal route, a theory premise, or proof
of an outcome. An intended theory says how the action is meant to reach the
desired outcome; each failure theory says how the same action could harm the
desired or a declared protected outcome and names a paired guardrail.

`reported-pattern` is authored context, not causal evidence. It can make a
matching failure theory easier to inspect, but cannot establish its mechanism,
upgrade a speculative concern, or replace the tool-derived review state. An
author-stated verdict is likewise an annotation beside—not a replacement for—
the computed verdict.
