# Gauge

Gauge is an elicitation instrument: it collects independent individual judgements
before discussion, reveals their spread, and can run a Delphi revision. A pooled
range is a receipt of room judgement, not evidence or a calibrated forecast.

Gauge may send revealed range questions to Fermi only as review-needed inputs. The
recipient must restate or explicitly adopt them before simulation. It never turns a
vote, pooled range, or discussion convergence into a Tree chance probability.

A disclosed aggregate belongs only to its exact frozen question set. Any edit to the
Gauge source withdraws the receipt permanently: undo may restore the template text,
but never revealed aggregate or participant data. A subsequently saved and reopened
document therefore has no receipt until a new session is disclosed.
